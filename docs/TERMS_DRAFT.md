# Prismy Studio / Project Ruby — proposed terms and acceptance

Version 0.1-draft · 2026-10-17 UTC · Not effective customer terms. This is a product drafting document, not a representation of legal approval. Do not put it behind a public “Accept” button until operator/contact details and applicable launch obligations have been reviewed and completed. The official content policy is proposed in AGE_AND_CONTENT_POLICY.md; its technical enforcement is not implemented in v0.4.8.

## 1. Scope and operator

These proposed terms cover official Prismy Studio / Project Ruby services, proprietary assets, downloads and any future creator marketplace. Operator legal name, address, support/privacy contact and effective date must be supplied before publication. “Companion Labs Ecosystem” is a proposed brand structure, not an assertion that a legal entity has been registered.

Open-source components are governed by their own licences. These terms do not restrict rights granted by GPL-2.0, MIT or other applicable open-source licences. Model and voice-asset licences are presented separately and must be followed. Purchasing software or a subscription does not transfer third-party copyrights, performer rights, trademarks or model ownership.

## 2. Eligibility and age presentation

The initial proposed official service is for eligible adults, at least 18 or the higher age required in the applicable location. You must provide truthful eligibility information. A self-declaration is not verified age. A future service for minors requires a separate compliant launch and must not be inferred from the availability of a cute character.

A Companion's age is fictional character information, separate from your real age. Voice-age categories describe reviewed creative presentation, not the performer's actual age. Select only supported, approved configurations. Relabelling a minor, childlike or ambiguous character as an adult does not permit mature treatment.

## 3. Content rules

The official product/services do not offer sexual or pornographic generation, explicit sexual roleplay, sexual voice performances, or sexualized depictions involving minors or childlike characters. Child sexualization, grooming, exploitation and sexual voice impersonation are prohibited regardless of payment, claimed ownership or fictional framing.

An optional mature-themes setting, if released, means nonsexual mature material and is not an official R/18 certification. It is restricted to eligible adults and suitable adult-presenting Companions. General mode remains the default. Appropriate non-erotic medical, educational and protective discussion is not prohibited merely because it concerns bodies, consent or abuse.

A subscription, custom voice, creator status, developer setting or ownership proposal does not create an exception to the official content rules. Do not bypass official service safeguards or distribute prohibited material through our storefront/services. These service/asset conditions do not purport to rewrite open-source software licences or control independent forks beyond rights and services we actually hold.

## 4. Voice, model and creator rights

Upload, train, clone, adapt or distribute a voice only when you possess the necessary rights and documented permissions from the relevant performer/rightsholders. Permission must cover the specific activities: synthesis/training, commercial outputs, model/voice-pack distribution and any adaptations or sublicensing you propose. We do not accept scraped anime/game dialogue or paid-service samples as evidence of those permissions.

Do not impersonate real people, misrepresent official affiliation or claim another creator's work as your own. You retain rights you actually own. If the marketplace launches, a separate creator agreement must define the limited hosting/distribution licence, moderation, takedown/dispute procedure, payouts, taxes and payment-provider obligations. A public creator badge does not itself certify identity or rights.

## 5. Privacy and computer actions

The product aims to perform local inference and store local identity/memory where supported. Internet search, external model endpoints, cloud processing, optional diagnostics and uploads may transmit data and require clear disclosures and appropriate separate controls. Terms acceptance is not blanket permission to transmit conversations, portable memory, reference recordings or private files. Do not claim all features are offline when an external route is enabled.

Any remote-service privacy notice must accurately state actual data categories, recipients, purposes, retention, deletion/export controls and contact details before launch. Voice recordings must not be reused to train shared models without specific permission. Desktop automation must use explicit, bounded permissions for consequential actions; installing the app is not blanket authorization over the user's computer.

## 6. Paid offerings and ownership proposals

The intended 80% free / 20% premium split is a product goal, not a guaranteed count of released features. Only the features, prices, limits and renewal/refund terms actually disclosed at checkout are purchased. No paid tier unlocks prohibited content.

The proposed marketplace model is the creator's designated listing amount plus a separately disclosed 2% buyer platform fee. Payment processing, taxes, currency conversion, refunds and chargebacks must be clearly allocated in the eventual creator/buyer agreements; no current statement guarantees net profit or a live payout service. Nothing here activates billing or promises unlimited cloud compute/download rights.

“Premium ownership” is not a current purchasable entitlement. Any later assignment or business licence requires a separate written agreement identifying exactly which transferable assets/rights are included. It cannot transfer rights the operator does not own, override law or third-party licences, or purchase an exception to the official child-safety/content rules.

## 7. Reliability, user responsibility and remedies

AI responses, translations, voice acting and age/personality presentation can be inaccurate. Do not rely on a Companion as a human professional or as an autonomous authority for consequential decisions. You are responsible for rights in your inputs and for how you use outputs; that statement does not eliminate the operator's legal responsibilities or your non-waivable consumer rights.

A final agreement must define lawful warranties/limitations, subscription cancellation and refunds, service suspension and any dispute/appeal mechanism. Do not invent a liability cap, arbitration clause, governing jurisdiction or waiver before those matters are actually selected and reviewed. Suspension of an official service must not be described as revocation of an independent open-source licence.

## 8. Acceptance, updates and reporting

Published terms must be accessible before acceptance and from Settings afterwards. Acceptance is explicit and unselected by default, versioned and recorded. Material changes require fresh acceptance before the affected future actions. Declining or withdrawing optional permissions must preserve existing user files and explain what features become unavailable.

Proposed required acknowledgement: “I meet the applicable age requirement and agree to the Terms of Service and Content Policy. I understand that paid access does not permit prohibited content.”

Separate optional controls: mature nonsexual themes (eligible adult configurations only), internet/cloud transfers, diagnostics and creator/voice rights agreements. None should be silently checked by accepting general terms. A final Privacy Notice must be linked and any required privacy consents collected separately.

Provide support, privacy, copyright/performer-rights and child-safety reporting contacts before public distribution of hosted services or the marketplace. The applicable handling process, retention and legal reporting duties must be reviewed; no response time or compliance certification is promised by this draft.
